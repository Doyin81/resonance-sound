About me

I am a Sound Engineer, Audio Production Specialist, and Technical Project Manager with a strong passion for delivering exceptional sound experiences through professional audio engineering, technical expertise, and creative problem-solving.

As the founder of Resonance Sound, I am committed to helping churches, event organisers, DJs, studios, and individuals achieve clear, balanced, powerful, and professional sound. My experience spans live sound engineering, church sound system installation and setup, audio production, livestream audio, recording, sound system configuration, and technical troubleshooting.

My approach combines technical knowledge with practical experience in managing audio equipment, configuring mixers and speaker systems, optimising microphones and monitors, managing subwoofers and crossovers, and troubleshooting sound-related challenges to achieve the best possible results.

Beyond sound engineering, I have experience leading technical teams, coordinating technical projects, and ensuring that audio systems are properly planned, configured, and operated. I understand that excellent sound is not simply about having quality equipment; it requires the right setup, proper gain staging, effective system management, attention to detail, and a clear understanding of the environment and audience.

Through Resonance Sound, my mission is to provide reliable, professional, and practical audio solutions that meet the unique needs of every client.

Our services

  • Professional live sound engineering and event audio support.
  • Church sound system setup, configuration, and optimisation.
  • Audio consultancy and sound system planning.
  • Training for sound engineers and technical teams.
  • Livestream audio setup and management.
  • Studio recording and audio production.
  • DJ and event sound system setup.
  • Microphone, mixer, amplifier, monitor, and speaker configuration.
  • Audio troubleshooting, system maintenance, and technical support.

Our commitment

At Resonance Sound, we believe that every voice deserves to be heard clearly and every sound experience deserves professional attention. We are committed to quality, technical excellence, continuous learning, and client satisfaction.

Our goal is to help clients get the best performance from their audio systems through professional service, practical knowledge, and solutions tailored to their needs.
